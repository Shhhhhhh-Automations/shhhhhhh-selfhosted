# Design System: shhhhhhh-selfhosted

## Typography
- **Primary Font**: Bricolage Grotesque
- **Weights**: Light (300), Regular (400), Medium (500), Bold (700)
- **Hierarchy**: Large, bold headings for impact; light, tracked-wide body text for "quiet" feel.

## Colors
- **Background**: Near-black (e.g., OKLCH 0.05 0.01 280)
- **Primary**: Neon Purple/Magenta (OKLCH 0.6 0.2 310)
- **Secondary**: Neon Cyan/Blue (OKLCH 0.7 0.15 230)
- **Accent**: Neon Green (OKLCH 0.8 0.2 145)
- **Neutrals**: Tinted toward brand hues.

## Layout & Components
- **Canvas**: A fluid, neon-lit background with a grid or organic shapes.
- **Buttons**: High contrast, pill-shaped. "Deploy Silently" (White/Black), "View Docs" (Black/White).
- **Cards**: Minimal, dark, with subtle neon borders or glows.

## Motion
- Fluid transitions for workflow lines.
- Subtle pulsing neon effects.
- Exponential ease-out for all movements.
